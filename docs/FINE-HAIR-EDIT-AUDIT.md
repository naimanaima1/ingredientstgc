## Claims that need stronger sourcing (or softer wording)
1. PVP gives rigid/stiff films on fine coily hair. Formulation-dependent; no study on fine coily hair. Keep as "likelier," as drafted.
2. Okra, flax, marshmallow, aloe, agave "film-forming." Mucilage/polysaccharide content is well established; hair-specific film or hold data for each is not cited. Pullulan, acacia gum, guar gum: general cosmetic-chemistry knowledge only.
3. Fine strands show deposit sooner / coat more easily. Plausible, uncited.
4. Glucoside shampoos leaving fine coily hair rough. Experience-based; not in refs.
5. Hemisqualane, squalane "lighter." Largely supplier claims.
6. Silicone buildup depends on type and washing. True in general; uncited.
7. "Fine hair isn't automatically high porosity." Reasonable; uncited.
8. Length-retention section (cut from web draft). No growth claims, but still uncited.

## Library wording (Ingredient Explorer)
Confirmed in the local build of the site (not the live site; I can't reach it from here):
- Ingredient pages show "Found in N products" and a per-product position, e.g. PVP "3 of 22", with a position strip.
- Product pages say e.g. "PVP is 3rd, 3rd of 22." Footnote: labels listed most to least, down to about 1%.
- Coverage: 86 hand-read labels. Several products (The Doux Bananas, Wet Line gel, Kinky-Curly 3-pack, Pattern custard, Doux Bee Girl) have NO label yet, so no position is shown.
- The only "top 5" wording is auto-generated text (scripts/ingredient_v2.py, "usually in the top 5"). I found no dedicated first-five filter or badge.
So the draft says: position shown per product, from labels read so far. It does not claim a first-five feature. Please check the live ingredient page.
